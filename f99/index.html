FAQ

Is there an official Mac app?

No. I wanted the official app, but I learned that it only runs on Windows. That’s why I made F99 Mood: so I could set up the lighting and music on my MacBook. It moves with music, and it’s free.

Can the keys move with music?

Yes, like AULA’s Windows app. On a Mac the keys follow whatever is playing, or the room through your microphone.

Is it free?

Yes. A star on GitHub would help me, though.

Does it work wirelessly?

Yes, after you set it up. Plug the keyboard in with a USB-C data cable to choose your lighting, and the keyboard saves it. Then you can switch to Bluetooth and the lighting stays. Live effects and music mode need the cable, because they send the lighting to the keyboard as it changes. Some cables only charge, and those won’t work.

Why does it ask for Input Monitoring and Accessibility?

Input Monitoring lets it talk to the keyboard over USB. Your Mac asks this of any app that connects to a keyboard. Accessibility is only for typing effects, which light the keys you press, and for macros that type text for you. You can say no to Accessibility and every other effect still works. The app never goes online, and nothing you type leaves your Mac.

Will it change my keyboard for good?

No. Settings › Restore original lighting puts back what your keyboard had before.

Is it made by AULA or EPOMAKER?

No. It’s an independent app, made by me. It isn’t connected to either company.
